What you'll learn

  • What SAP FSCD does and where it sits next to policy, claims and finance
  • The building blocks: business partner, contract account, posting document, open item
  • How premiums are billed, collected, chased and cleared
  • How claims, refunds and commission are paid out
  • How customers pay in Denmark, Sweden and Norway
  • How reminders, bounced payments, payment plans and write-offs work
  • How FSCD postings reach the general ledger and are reconciled at month-end
  • How SAP BRIM bills pay-per-use, subscription and partner products next to FSCD
  • The screens, tables and questions a BA uses every day

Who this course is for

Business analysts, testers and new team members on insurance projects who need to understand SAP FSCD quickly. No SAP experience is needed. Each lesson takes 15 to 25 minutes and ends with a 10-question test that changes every time you take it.

FSCD Academy. Names, numbers, codes and screens in the lessons are illustrative examples for learning, not real data or real SAP screenshots.
