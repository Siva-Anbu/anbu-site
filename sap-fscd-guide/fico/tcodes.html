Frequently used T-codes in FICO

A lookup list of the transaction codes (T-codes) you will use most often in SAP FI and CO, grouped by topic. Keep this page open next to your SAP system. Reference only, there is no test on this page.

How to use this page

Type part of a code or a word (for example "payment" or "KS") in the box to narrow the lists. Codes and descriptions come from general SAP knowledge and public lists. They can differ by release and by project, so confirm each one in your own system (SE93 shows the official description) and ask a colleague if unsure. Check which codes your role is allowed to use.

Good to know

S/4HANA stores FI and CO postings together in one table, ACDOCA (the Universal Journal). Several older reports and codes still exist next to the newer ones, so the same question can often be answered in two ways. Ask your team which one your project uses.
