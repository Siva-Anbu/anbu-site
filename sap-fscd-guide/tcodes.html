Frequently used T-codes in FSCD

A lookup list of the transaction codes (T-codes) you will use most often in SAP FSCD (FS-CD), grouped by topic. Keep this page open next to your SAP system. Reference only, there is no test on this page.

How to use this page

Type part of a code or a word (for example "payment" or "KS") in the box to narrow the lists. Codes and descriptions come from general SAP knowledge and public lists. They can differ by release and by project, so confirm each one in your own system (SE93 shows the official description) and ask a colleague if unsure. Check which codes your role is allowed to use.

Nothing matches. Try a shorter word.

1. Business partner and contract account

A business partner is the person or company. A contract account holds that partner's billing and payment settings.

T-codeWhat it doesWhen you would use it
BPCreate, change and display a business partner (policyholder, broker, claimant)Check an address or bank details
CAA1Create a contract accountSet up the account for one policyholder's billing and payments
CAA2Change a contract accountChange a payment method or the dunning procedure
CAA3Display a contract accountSee which payment method and bank account a customer uses
FPP1 / FPP2 / FPP3Create / change / display a contract partner (older transactions from before BP)You may meet these in old notes. In a current system use BP

2. Posting and document processing

Every posting in FSCD is a document with items. These codes show you the documents, the balance and the open items.

T-codeWhat it doesWhen you would use it
FPL9Account balance: all items of a contract accountAlways the first stop for "why does this customer owe this amount?"
FPE3Display one posting documentOpen a document and read its items, dates and posting reason
FPE1Create (post) a document by handTest data or a manual correction, usually operations work
FPE2Change a posted document (only the fields that are allowed to change)A small correction without reversing the document
FP08Reverse a document (FP08M for a mass reversal)Undo a wrong posting. Confirm with your team how reversals are done in your project
FPO1Open item list at a key dateReconciliation: compare open items at month-end with the ledger
FPCJCash deskCustomer service books a payment taken in person
FPCJRCash desk evaluationCheck what the cash desk booked

3. Payments in and out

Incoming premiums, direct debits, returns and payouts.

T-codeWhat it doesWhen you would use it
FP05Process a payment lot (a batch of incoming payments)Post payments from a bank file or statement
FPCPLPayment clarification worklistWork through payments that could not be matched to a customer
FPY1Payment run (direct debit, card and reference-based payments)The run that builds the collection files and claim payouts
FP03MMass run: release items for collectionRelease due items so they can be collected
FP03LList of collection itemsSee which items are on their way to collection
FP09Process a returns lot (bounced or rejected payments)Post failed direct debits sent back by the bank
FPCRLReturns clarification worklistWork through returns that could not be matched
FPM4Returns historySee the returns for a customer or account

4. Dunning, instalment plans and write-offs

Reminders for late payers, payment plans and closing debts that will not be paid.

T-codeWhat it doesWhen you would use it
FPVADunning proposal runFind out who should get a reminder and at which level
FPVBDunning activity runSend the reminders and post the dunning fees
FPM3Dunning history (descriptions vary between sources, check in your system)Which reminders did this customer get, and when?
FPR1 / FPR2 / FPR3Create / change / display an instalment planSet up a payment plan for a customer in difficulty
FP04Write off items (FP04M for a mass write-off, FP04H for the history)Close a debt that will never be collected

5. General ledger and month-end

FSCD is a sub-ledger. It sends totals to the general ledger, and finance checks they agree.

T-codeWhat it doesWhen you would use it
FPG1Transfer FSCD totals to the general ledgerThe batch job (often nightly) that creates the ledger postings
FPG4Close reconciliation keys in a mass runClosed keys are ready for the transfer
FPG5Show which general ledger documents belong to FSCD documentsTrace an FSCD posting into the ledger
Good to know

The normal FI display transaction FB03 does not show FSCD documents directly. Use FPE3 to read the FSCD document, and FPG5 to find the ledger document it ended up in.

6. Helper transactions and tables

Tools for looking things up and for checking data. Read-only use is the safe habit.

T-codeWhat it doesWhen you would use it
SE16NBrowse table data (read-only)Quick data checks. Ask before using it in production
SE93Look up a transaction code and its descriptionConfirm what a code does in your system
SLG1Application logRead the messages of a run, for example the GL transfer
SM37Job overviewCheck whether the nightly jobs ran
SU53Last authorization checkFind out which permission was missing after a "not authorized" message
FKKVK / FKKVKPTables: contract accounts / link between partner and accountFind all accounts of one business partner
DFKKKO / DFKKOPTables: document header / document itemsList open premium items for a report requirement
DFKKZPTable: payment lot itemsFind where an unmatched payment is parked
