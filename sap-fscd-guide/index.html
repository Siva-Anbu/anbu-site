How the notes are laid out

  • Short lessons of 15 to 25 minutes, in plain English
  • One made-up Danish example followed from start to finish
  • Simple diagrams of the main flows
  • A 10-question self-test after each lesson, so I can check my own understanding
  • Progress saved in your browser only, lesson by lesson
