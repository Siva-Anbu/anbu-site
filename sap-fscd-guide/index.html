How every course works

  • Short lessons of 15 to 25 minutes, in plain English
  • One Danish example followed from start to finish
  • Diagrams of every flow and walk-throughs of the key screens
  • A 10-question test after every lesson that changes every time
  • Progress saved in your browser, lesson by lesson
  • A certificate when you finish a course

Who these courses are for

Business analysts, testers and new team members on SAP projects who need to understand SAP finance quickly. No SAP experience is needed. Start with the course that matches your project, or take both: FSCD is built on SAP contract accounting and posts into the FICO general ledger, so the two courses fit together.

SAP Academy. Names, numbers, codes and screens in the lessons are illustrative examples for learning, not real data or real SAP screenshots.
