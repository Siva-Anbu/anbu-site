What you'll learn

  • What SAP FI and CO do, and how a company is set up in SAP
  • GL accounts, debit and credit, and how to read a journal entry
  • How the Universal Journal (ACDOCA) brings FI and CO together
  • Procure to pay: purchase orders, goods receipt, invoice matching and the payment run
  • Order to cash: billing, VAT, incoming payments, bank statements and dunning
  • Controlling: cost centres, internal orders, allocations and profitability
  • Fixed assets, depreciation and the month-end close
  • The screens, reports and questions a FICO business analyst uses every day

Who this course is for

Business analysts, testers and new team members who need to understand SAP finance quickly. No SAP or accounting experience is needed. Each lesson takes 15 to 25 minutes and ends with a 10-question test that changes every time you take it.

FICO Academy. Names, numbers, codes and screens in the lessons are illustrative examples for learning, not real data or real SAP screenshots.
