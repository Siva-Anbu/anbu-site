What you'll learn

  • What SAP FI and CO do at an insurer, next to the policy, claims and FS-CD systems
  • GL accounts, debit and credit, and how to read a journal entry
  • How the Universal Journal (ACDOCA) brings FI and CO together
  • Buying and paying suppliers, and why an insurer cannot deduct VAT
  • How premiums flow from FS-CD into the ledger: insurance tax, payments, written and earned premium
  • Costs by line of business and the loss, expense and combined ratios
  • Fixed assets, premium and claims reserves (RBNS, IBNR) and the month-end close
  • The screens, reports and questions a FICO business analyst at an insurer uses every day

Who this course is for

Business analysts, testers and new team members in insurance who need to understand SAP finance quickly. No SAP or accounting experience is needed. The examples follow Nordica Forsikring A/S, a made-up Danish non-life insurer. Each lesson takes 15 to 25 minutes and ends with a 10-question test that changes every time you take it.

FICO Notes. Names, numbers, codes and screens in the lessons are illustrative examples for learning, not real data or real SAP screenshots.
